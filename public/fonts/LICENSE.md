# Font licences

Grana self-hosts four families. None of them is Grana's to relicense — each is redistributed here
under its own terms, and this file travels with the files because one of them requires it to.

| Family | Weights here | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cabinet Grotesk | 500, 700, 800 | [Fontshare](https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/cabinet-grotesk) (Indian Type Foundry) | ITF Free Font Licence — free for personal and commercial use, including web embedding |
| General Sans | 400, 500, 600 | [Fontshare](https://www.fontshare.com/fonts/general-sans) (Indian Type Foundry) | ITF Free Font Licence — same terms |
| Spline Sans Mono | 400, 500 | [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Spline+Sans+Mono) (Eben Sorkin, Mirko Velimirović) | SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 |
| Source Serif 4 | 400, 500 | [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Source+Serif+4) (Frank Grießhammer, Adobe) | SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 |

The two OFL families may be redistributed, modified and bundled, provided this notice goes with
them and they are not sold on their own. The full text of the SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 is at
<https://openfontlicense.org>.

The `.woff2` files here are latin subsets, converted for the web. Replacing or adding a weight means
updating `registry/grana/styles/fonts.css` and this table together — a face that ships without its
licence named is a face nobody downstream can safely use.
