Revenue Farm is a neutral multi-tenant tool for the people who install: dense, daily, flat.
**No brand hue anywhere in the chrome** — the shell is warm neutral (a stone-100 canvas, white
cards, hairlines), the customer's logo is the only identity on screen, and colour means
**status or data only**. The product surface carries **no shadows at all**: structure comes
from hairlines. The voice is General Sans 500 (Cabinet Grotesk is banned inside the product;
it belongs to the marketing site). Numbers are the brand: monospaced, tabular,
Italian-formatted (`1.234,56`, `€ 18.400`, `28/07/2026`), set with the `num` utility. Copy is
Italian, sentence case, short declaratives, no exclamation marks; no italics anywhere.

The workspace is **one large white card** on the canvas: a transparent sidebar, then the
inset content card (14px radius, hairline) holding a topbar and a scrolling page. Every
AI/system action is labelled — a person must always tell at a glance what the machine did.
The marketing surface (`data-surface="marketing"` on a wrapper) is the expressive opposite:
ecru paper, unit tints, framed imagery, one dark band as punctuation — never the product.
